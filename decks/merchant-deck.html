How Attest Works

Your side

Install the SDK. It detects agent traffic and issues identity challenges automatically.

Agent side

Agents respond with cryptographic tokens proving who built them and who they act for.

Verified agents get loyalty pricing, real-time inventory, no CAPTCHAs.
Agents want to identify themselves. You just need to ask.

Three layers of value

Detect. Challenge. Verify.

LayerRequires agent cooperationWhat you learn
