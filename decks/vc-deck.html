The problem

Merchants Are Flying Blind

AI agents are shopping, browsing, and buying. Every agent looks the same to the merchant: an anonymous HTTP request.

  • Who sent this? A loyal customer? A competitor scraping prices? A fraud bot?
  • What is it authorized to do? Browse only? Purchase up to $500? Unlimited?
  • Is this a returning customer? Should I apply loyalty pricing?

Merchants choose between blocking all bots (losing real customers) or letting everything through (inviting abuse).
